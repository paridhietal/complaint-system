Subject: URGENT - Adverse Reaction Report - Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules

From: Dr. Rakesh Mehta, Sunrise Hospital Pharmacy, Mumbai
Date: 03-Sep-2026

We are reporting a serious adverse event involving Amoxicillin 500mg Capsules,
Batch/Lot No. AMX-2026-0417, manufactured 12-Jan-2026, expiry 11-Jan-2028.

A patient (age 34) developed a severe allergic skin reaction (widespread rash
and swelling) within 2 hours of taking two capsules from this batch. The
patient required emergency treatment. On inspection, several capsules from
the affected strip appeared discolored (yellow-brown instead of white) and
had an unusual odor.

Approximately 4 kg (about 8,000 capsules) of this batch remain in our pharmacy
stock and have been quarantined pending your investigation. We request an
urgent root cause investigation and CAPA, as this may indicate a
manufacturing or storage-related contamination issue.

Please treat this as highest priority given the direct patient safety impact.
